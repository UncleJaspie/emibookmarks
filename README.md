# EMI Bookmarks

### Advanced Crafting Planning, Recipe Management & Bookmarks for EMI

**Ever spent hours planning a massive crafting project, only to realize you need your Recipe Tree for something else?**

EMI Bookmarks was created out of frustration with the limitations of EMI's Recipe Tree system, particularly in large, complex modpacks where microcrafting, resource management, and long-term crafting plans are essential.

While EMI provides a powerful recipe viewer and crafting planner, its Recipe Tree system primarily revolves around managing one active crafting plan at a time. Combined with limited bookmark organization, this can make juggling multiple projects unnecessarily difficult.

**EMI Bookmarks aims to change that.**

Inspired by the advanced bookmark management system of **GregTech New Horizons' NEI (GTNH NEI)**, EMI Bookmarks brings a dedicated, separate bookmark sidebar to EMI, combining the organizational strengths of GTNH NEI with EMI's Recipe Tree functionality.

## Features

### Advanced Bookmark Organization

*   Organize bookmarks and recipes into dedicated groups.
*   Arrange and reorder bookmarks to suit your crafting workflow.
*   Keep multiple crafting projects organized without cluttering your active Recipe Tree.
*   Use a dedicated sidebar inspired by GTNH NEI.

### Save Multiple Recipe Trees

*   Turn your active EMI Recipe Tree into a persistent, organized crafting plan.
*   Save entire Recipe Trees as recipe groups directly in the bookmark sidebar.
*   Create and manage multiple independent crafting plans.
*   Free up the active Recipe Tree to plan your next project without losing previous work.

### Revisit, Edit & Refine Your Plans

*   Reopen previously saved Recipe Trees whenever you need them.
*   Continue modifying and expanding existing crafting plans.
*   Configure preferred recipes and recipe providers to ensure your crafting trees follow your intended production methods.
*   Switch between projects without having to rebuild your plans from scratch.

## Built for Complex Modpacks

Whether you're planning a massive GregTech multiblock, organizing hundreds of intermediate components, or simply trying to keep track of several crafting projects at once, EMI Bookmarks helps make complex crafting more manageable.

**Your Recipe Tree should be a planning tool, not a limitation.**

EMI Bookmarks brings together the familiarity of GTNH NEI's bookmark management and the power of EMI's Recipe Tree system, allowing you to plan, save, organize, revisit, and refine your crafting projects without sacrificing your existing progress.

**Plan more. Organize better. Never lose track of your next big craft.**  
  
  
  
EMI Bookmarks doesn't automatically solve EMI's recipe-selection quirks. Instead, it allows players to select their preferred recipes and save those choices as part of their crafting plans. That's an important difference, especially for GregTech players who frequently encounter multiple ways to produce the same item.
